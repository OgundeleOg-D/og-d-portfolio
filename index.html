About Apex

Built with the discipline of engineering. Finished with the character of craftsmanship.

Apex Iron & Oak was formed around a simple premise: structural rigor and material warmth aren't opposing forces — they're the two disciplines every serious build requires. Our commercial teams bring the precision of steel-frame engineering; our residential teams bring the patience of fine timber work. The same firm, the same standards, applied across every scale of project.

Every engagement — a 45,000 sq ft commercial shell or a custom Hill Country residence — runs through the same estimating discipline, the same daily site documentation, and the same senior oversight. That consistency is the actual product.
