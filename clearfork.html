The Clearfork Commercial Center

Fort Worth, Texas 45,000 Sq Ft Steel + Glass Development
Project Overview

A 45,000 sq ft steel & glass development in Fort Worth's Clearfork district.

The Clearfork Commercial Center is a ground-up commercial development combining a steel-frame structural system with an extensive glass envelope — a pairing that put Apex's structural and envelope coordination discipline to work end to end.

As a commercial development, the project moved through coordinated structural, envelope, and MEP execution under a single point of accountability, consistent with the estimating and site-documentation standard Apex applies across every project scale.

Clearfork Commercial CenterAlternate elevation, Fort Worth
The Challenge

Coordinating structure and envelope at commercial scale.

A steel-and-glass commercial development at this scale requires close coordination between the structural frame, the building envelope, and MEP systems — sequencing that leaves little room for error once steel is set and glazing begins. Projects of this type call for disciplined scheduling, transparent budgeting, and daily site documentation so that structural, envelope, and interior trades can move in sequence without rework. That coordination discipline — not any single specialized technique — is what Apex's project management approach is built around.
