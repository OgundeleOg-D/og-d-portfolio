Westlake Custom Estate

Westlake, Texas Modern Hill Country Residence Custom Design-Build
Project Overview

A modern Hill Country-influenced luxury residence in Westlake.

Westlake Custom Estate is a full architectural design-build, carried by Apex's residential team from foundation to final finish carpentry as a single, senior-overseen engagement rather than a series of disconnected trades.

As a custom residential project, it followed the same estimating discipline and daily site documentation Apex applies to its commercial work — the patience of fine timber craftsmanship paired with the same structural rigor.

Westlake Custom EstateAlternate elevation, Westlake, TX
Architectural Direction

Modern geometry, natural Texas materials.

The estate's direction draws on modern Hill Country design — clean geometric massing paired with natural materials such as stone, timber, steel, and glass. The design integrates indoor and outdoor living, with the architecture opening toward the surrounding landscape rather than standing apart from it.
